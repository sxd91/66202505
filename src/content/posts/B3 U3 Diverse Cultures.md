# B3 U3 Diverse Cultures Language Points

## 1. admit
**vt. & vi.** 承认；**vt.** 准许进入(或加入) → **n.** 承认；允许进入(加入)；录取 → **n.** 进入权；入场权 → **adv.** 诚然；确实地；不可否认地

*   **vt.** 容许...进入；**vt.** 准许...加入(俱乐部，组织)；接收入学/入院
    只有持票者才可进入体育场。
    (1) Only ticket-holders will **be admitted to** the stadium.
    (2) I shall be very likely to **be admitted to** my dream university.

*   **被收治入院**
    (3) You think you'll be admitted to Peking University? I think you should **admit** you are wrong. （被收治入院）

*   **vt & vi 承认**；**承认(做)某事**
    (4) We should have the courage to **admit** our mistakes.
    (5) I had to **admit to being** nervous about the test.

*   **vt & vi 供认**
    (6) He refused to **admit to** the other charges. 他拒绝供认其他指控。
    (7) She **admitted stealing** the car. 她承认自己偷了车。

*   **普遍认为**
    (8) It is generally **admitted** that physical exercise in childhood can be beneficial to people.

## 2. occur
**vi.** 发生；出现 (p.t./p.p. **occurred**) → **n.** **occurrence** 发生；出现；事件
(1) When exactly did the incident **occur**?
(2) Cultural differences **occur** wherever you go. (出现；存在)

*   **(主意或想法) 被想到**
    *   被某人想到 ________ 某人想到做某事 ________
    *   某人突然想起…… ________
    (3) It never **occurred** to him that he might be taken in.
    (4) Not once **did it occur** to Michael that he could one day become a top player in his team.

*   **辨析 occur, happen, take place**
    *   **相同点：** ① **发生** ② **多指偶然发生 / 无计划**
    *   **不同点：**
        *   **occur**：**occur to 想起**
        *   **happen**：**happen to 碰巧**
             Maybe something unexpected happened.
        *   **take place**：**按计划进行**

## 3. seek
p.t./p.p. **sought** → **-ing** ______
寻找；追求 ________ 找出某人/某物 ________
试图做某事 ________

(1) On the weekend, parents always take their children to the nearby countryside to **seek** nature. （在大自然中寻求舒适，安逸）
(2) The police **sought** the suspect before he could sneak away. （搜寻）
(3) I **sought** to provide a valuable contribution to the field of history, but in vain. （试图）
*   被大众追求的；难求的 ________ 捉迷藏 ________
*   闯荡；闯天下 ________
(4) By the mid-1920s, she had become one of Broadway's most **sought-after** actresses. (被大众追求的；难求的)
(5) I was the one in **seeking** that you never came looking for and I hid for hours from no one.... (捉迷藏)
(6) He finally made up his mind to **seek his fortune** overseas. (闯荡；闯天下)

## 4. earn
**vt. & vi.** 挣得；赚得；赢得；博得 → **n. (pl.)** 收入；收益
*   赚钱/取得成功/挣得一个好名声 ________ 谋生 ________
*   使某人赢得/获得...________ 来之不易的 ________
(1) Ordinary people tend to spend their whole life to **earn a living**. (赚钱/取得成功/挣得一个好名声)
(2) Playing piano in the jazz bar is his way to **earn money**. (谋生)
(3) Practice meditation regularly **earns** me the inner peace I've been longing for. (获得内心的平静)
(4) Your reputation is **hard-earned** . (来之不易的), Don't do something stupid to jeopardize it.
(5) I used up all my **earnings** to purchase a 3-storeyed house with a tiny garden.

## 5. select
**vt.** (经过认真思考而) 挑选；精选
*   select sb / sth **as** sth (被动) ________
*   select sb / sth **from** sth (被动) ________
*   select sb / sth **to do** (被动) ________
*   **vt.** 选择；挑选；选拔 → **n.** **selection** 选择性的；有选择的；择优的 → **n.** **select** 挑选；选择；选拔
(1) He selected a mobile phone for personal use.
(2) He had hopes of being **selected for** the national team.
(3) Mr. Clark was **selected as** the Best Teacher of the Year.
(4) He's the only student in the class that has been **selected to** take part in the Model United Nations conference.

## 7. bring about
*   抚养长大 ________ 提出 ________
*   使...显现 / 使出版 ________ 赚得 / 引进；引入 ________
*   减少；降低；推翻 ________
(1) What do you suppose **brings about** the diversity of the Chinatown?
(2) I was **brought up** by my grandparents in the downtown area.
(3) I had to seek a chance to **bring up** this matter during the meeting.
(4) Hardships can **bring out** your super power.
(5) The author claimed that he **brought out** 2 books last year.
(6) This job **brings in** 5000 yuan a month, which means I can earn 60000 per year.
(7) We were able to **bring in** advanced technology from another company.
(8) I'll **bring down** the price a little before I buy the suit.

## 7. claim
**v.** 声称做/做过某事 ________ 声称/断言 ________
按保险索赔 ________
(1) 有些科学家们宣称攻克癌症已有重大的突破。
    Some scientists are **claiming to have made** a major breakthrough in the fight against cancer.
(2) Don't **claim to know** what you don't know.
(3) 他不敢自称专家，一直谦虚地微笑着。
    He doesn't **claim to be** an expert, smiling modestly all the time.
(4) Some students **claimed to have seen** a building under construction is a new library with modern equipment.
(5) 如果你度假时出了车祸，可按保险索赔。
    If you had a car accident on your holiday, you could **claim on the insurance**.
(6) A lot of lost property is **unclaimed**. 许多失物从未被认领。
(7) 在过去的100年间，地震夺去了超过一百六十万人的生命。
    Earthquakes have **claimed** more than 1.6 million lives in the last hundred years.

*   **n.** 提出要求 ________ (对事物的) 权利；所有权 ________
(8) 他说他清白，谁也不相信。
    Nobody believed his **claim** that he was innocent.
(9) Although the clerk explained that the loss of their houses was due to the occurrence of the earthquake, the residents still **claimed compensation** for the loss. 提出损害赔偿要求
(10) The construction workers are on strike **to claim** higher wages. 提出加薪要求
(11) 他的金融公司的所有权是无效的。
    His **claim to** ownership of the finance company is invalid.

## 8. apart from
(1) **Apart from** English, Maria speaks four other languages fluently.
(2) **Apart from** the fact that it was raining and they had to bring out the folded chairs, the picnic was a success.
(3) I've finished everything **apart from** the last question.
(4) **Apart from** the spelling mistakes, your essay is well put.

## 9. escape
**v.** 从...逃走 ________ 逃避做某事 ________
sth escape sb ________
(1) The hunter tried to **escape from** the forest, but he lost his way.
(2) The thief tiptoed into the house, **escaping the notice of** the house owner. 逃过屋主的注意
(3) You are lucky to **escape being injured**.
(4) He **escaped** being killed. 他险些丧命。
(5) She looks familiar, but **her name escapes me**. (= I can't remember her name)
(6) A small hole allowed smoke to **escape** to the top.

*   **n.** 逃跑 ________ 九死一生 ________
    消防通道 ________
(7) For her, travel was **an escape from** the boredom of her everyday life. 逃避...
(8) He had **a narrow escape**. 死里逃生/九死一生
(9) Can you smell an **escape** of gas?
(10) Every building should have **an escape route**. 消防通道

## 10. 分数的用法
①分子在**前**，分母在**后**；②分母在**前**，分子在**后**
*   三分之一：**one-third** 百分之一：**one hundredth**
*   三分之二：**two-thirds** 四分之一：**one-fourth**
*   十分之一：**one-tenth** 四分之三：**three-fourths**
*   1小时15分钟：**one hour and fifteen minutes**

*   **分数+名词作主语的主谓一致**
(1) 氧气只占空气的五分之一。
    Only one-fifth of air **consists** of oxygen.
(2) 大约三分之二的学生都将参加会议。
    About two thirds of the students **are** going to attend the meeting.

## 11. settle
**vt.&vi.** 定居;结束(争论);解决(纠纷) → **n.** 定居;解决;协议;和解 → **a.** 不大可能改变的;固定的;定居者
**vi. & vi.** 坐下；(使)处于舒适的位置
(1) They **sat** at a comfortable cafe and within minutes, another customer was approaching their table.
(2) She **settled into** her chair and started to read.
(3) She **settled down** in Vienna after her father's death.
(4) This region **was settled** by the Dutch in the 19th century.
(5) You are expected to have the ability to **settle** an argument / a dispute/ a problem. 解决争论/纠纷/问题
(6) It was **settled** that they would get married in June.

*   **短语拓展：**
    *   安顿下来；适应（新工作） **settle down**； (使) 安顿下来；定居 **settle down**； 专心致力于（做）某事 **settle down to**
(1) It always takes the class a while to **settle down** at the start of the lesson.
(2) A butterfly **settled on** a red flower.
(3) Back in the hotel, an air of sadness **settled over** the group.
(4) The house had been empty for years, and dust had **settled on** all the furniture.

## 12. contain
**vt.** 包含;含有;容纳 → **n.** 容器
(1) This drink doesn't **contain** any alcohol.
*   **区别：** contain 包含全部内容； include 包含部分内容
*   The container **contains** many kinds of fruits, **including** apples.
(2) The mobile cabin hospitals can **contain** thousands of beds to treat infected patients.
(3) Hearing the news, he couldn't **contain** himself and burst into laughter.

## 13. 不定代词，数词，介词短语 + which/whom (复习)
(1) 工厂有二百个工人，其中女性占三分之二。
    There are 200 workers in the factory, **two-thirds of whom** are women.
(2) 能与之交朋友的人应该是正派的。
    Persons **with whom** you can make friends should be decent.
(3) 他找不到任何可以过夜的旅馆。
    He could not find any hotel **to stay at** for the night.
    He could not find any hotel **at which** he could stay for the night.

## 14. 独立主格复习
(1) **All things considered**, the planned trip will have to be called off.
    A. considered  B. be considered  C. considering  D. having considered
    答案：**A**
(2) **All flights having been canceled** because of the snowstorm, many passengers could do nothing but take the train.
    A. had been canceled  B. have been canceled  C. were canceled  D. having been canceled
    答案：**D**
(3) **So many directors being absent**, the board meeting had to be put off.
    A. were absent  B. being absent  C. had been absent  D. being absent
    答案：**B**
(4) **All the tasks fulfilled** ahead of time, they decided to go on holiday for a week.
    A. been fulfilled  B. having been fulfilled  C. were fulfilled  D. had been fulfilled
    答案：**C**
(5) **The speech having been delivered**, a lively discussion started.
    A. being delivered  B. was delivered  C. be delivered  D. having been delivered
    答案：**D**
(6) Silver is the best conductor of electricity, copper **to follow** it closely.
    A. followed  B. following  C. to follow  D. being following
    答案：**C**
(7) The sale usually takes place outside the house, with the audience **seated** on benches, chairs or boxes.
    A. having seated  B. seating  C. seated  D. having been seated
    答案：**C**
(8) **Having suffered** such heavy pollution already, it may now be too late to clean up the river.
    A. Having suffered  B. Suffering  C. To suffer  D. Suffered
    答案：**A**
(9) The fish **tasted** bad, the children left much of it.
    A. tasted, untouched  B. tasting, untouched  C. tasting, untouching  D. tasted, untouching
    答案：**B**
(10) **Judging from** what you say, he ought to succeed.
    A. Judged  B. Judging  C. When you judged  D. Because you judge
    答案：**B**
(11) Everything **taken** into consideration, they ought to have another chance.
    A. to take  B. taken  C. to be taken  D. taking
    答案：**B**
