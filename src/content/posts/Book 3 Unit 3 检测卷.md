## 重点语法解答
### 11. settle
vt.&vi. 定居;结束(争论);解决(纠纷) → settlement n. 定居;解决;协议;和解 → settler n. 定居者
(1) They **sat** at a comfortable cafe and within minutes, another customer was approaching their table.
(2) She **settled into** her chair and started to read.
(3) She **settled down** in Vienna after her father's death.
(4) This region **was settled** by the Dutch in the 19th century.
(5) You are expected to have the ability to **settle** an argument / a dispute/ a problem.
(6) It was **settled** that they would get married in June.
短语拓展：安顿下来；适应（新工作）settle down； (使)安顿下来；定居 settle down； 专心致力于（做）某事 settle down to (doing) sth.
### 12. contain
vt. 包含;含有;容纳 → container n. 容器
(1) This drink doesn't **contain** any alcohol.
区别: contain 指包含全部内容/成分； include 指包含部分内容
The container **contains** many kinds of fruits, **including** apples / apples.
(2) The mobile cabin hospitals can **contain** thousands of beds to treat infected patients.
(3) Hearing the news, he couldn't **contain** himself and burst into laughter.

### 13. 不定代词、数词、介词短语 + which/whom(复习)
(1) 工厂有二百个工人，其中女性占三分之二。
There are 200 workers in the factory, **two-thirds of whom** are women.
(2) 能与之交朋友的人应该是正派的。
Persons **with whom** you can make friends should be decent.
(3) 他找不到任何可以过夜的旅馆。
He could not find any hotel **to stay at** for the night. (不定式作定语)
He could not find any hotel **at which** he could stay for the night. (介词+关系代词)

### 14. 独立主格复习
(1) **All things considered**, the planned trip will have to be called off. 选 A
(2) **All flights having been canceled** because of the snowstorm, many passengers could do nothing but take the train. 选 D
(3) **So many directors being absent**, the board meeting had to be put off. 选 B
(4) **All the tasks fulfilled** ahead of time, they decided to go on holiday for a week. 选 C
(5) **The speech having been delivered**, a lively discussion started. 选 D
(6) Silver is the best conductor of electricity, copper **to follow** it closely. 选 C
(7) The sale usually takes place outside the house, with the audience **seated** on benches, chairs or boxes. 选 D
(8) **Having suffered** such heavy pollution already, it may now be too late to clean up the river. 选 A
(9) The fish **tasted** bad, the children left much of it. 选 B
(10) **Judging from** what you say, he ought to succeed. 选 B
(11) Everything **taken** into consideration, they ought to have another chance. 选 B
