## 五、本单元语法填空重点词汇训练
1. (diverse) There are **diverse** cultures in our country, but it's the **diversity** that makes our motherland wonderful.
2. (infect) The **infection** spread quickly through the body. She was infected **by** the COVID-19 virus.
3. (fortune) At the age of four, she fell into an icy river and was **fortunately** rescued by some local villagers. My cousin was **fortunate** to find a job after graduation. Three immigrants headed to California to seek their **fortune**. **Unfortunately**, the event has been canceled due to bad weather.
4. (admit) One day I got word that he was **admitted to** a hospital for a serious disease. To my **admission**, the exhibition is just so-so. Afterwards, he had to **admit putting** some poisonous mushrooms in the soup. *(答案句)* I am writing to offer my sincere congratulations to you **that you have been admitted to Peking University.**
5. (immigrate) The government has introduced new policies to control **immigration**. The **immigrants** quickly adapted to the new environment. His family immigrated from Italy **to** the United States.
6. (select) The store has a wide **selection** of fruits. Teachers should be **selected** about the materials they use in the classroom. They selected him **as** the team leader because of his leadership skills.
7. (finance) The company is facing **financial** difficulties. They are doing well **financially**.
8. (poem) The famous poet has written many wonderful **poems**. Li Bai is one of the most famous **poets** in Chinese history. He has a deep love for **poetry** and often reads it in his spare time.
9. (poison) Some mushrooms are extremely **poisonous** and can kill you if eaten. The river **was poisoned** by industrial waste.
10. (collect) He loves **collecting** stamps and he often shows off his **collection** in front of others. We need to collect data **from** various sources for this research.
11. (settle) Early **settlers** in America had to face many hardships. After years of traveling, he finally **settled down** and got married. As far as I'm concerned, the problems can **be settled** in no time if you double your efforts. It’s been an honor to watch the panda programme develop and to see the pandas **settle into** their new home.
12. (construct) The **construction** of the skyscraper took several years. He gave us some **constructive** suggestions on how to improve our work. After the earthquake, they had to **construct** their houses.
13. (suit) I don't have anything **suitable** to wear for the party, so I must buy an evening dress that **suits** me perfectly. These books are **suitable** for children under 10.
14. (seek) Mike **sought** help from his friends when he was in trouble, but they turned him down. He **sought** chances to renew friendships. We yelled at the bear and hit glass bottles, **seeking** to frighten the bear away. This project seeks **to combine** traditional art with modern technology.
15. (escape) It is said that he narrowly escaped **being killed** in the traffic accident the other day. It was claimed that a prisoner had escaped **from** the prison, which shocked the public. Luckily, we nine died in the accident, **fortunately escaping** being hurt by the hungry polar bear.
16. (earn) He **earned** three thousand dollars and gives most of his **earnings** to his wife.
17. (contain) First announced in April, 2016, the tax which applies to soft drinks **containing** more than 5g of sugar per 100ml, was introduced to help reduce childhood obesity. The class experiment that day was to show how heating a **container** of water would bring air bubbling to the surface.
18. (occur) That night a terrible storm **occurred** with the wind blowing and beating the house. A good idea **occurred to me** that I could go downtown to buy some souvenirs after the mission. **It occurred to her that** she had left the tent in the hotel where we stayed the night before.

## 六、本单元重点句式仿写训练
**句型1：what引导的感叹句**
1. What important information you've given me!
2. What a lovely day it is today!
3. What beautiful flowers they are!
4. How fast the time flies!
5. What a clever boy he is!
仿写：
1. **What an interesting story he told!**
2. **How clever these children are!**
3. **What beautiful flowers these are!**
4. **How happily they are playing!**

**句型2：不定式作目的的状语（表目的）**
1. I decided to do something **to cheer** him up.
2. These sepals open on warm days **to give** the inside plants sunshine and fresh air.
3. Although parks of all sizes and types exist at any level, **to catch** our attention because of their large size and variety.
4. People have been pouring into stadiums since the days of ancient Greece. In around 80 A.D., the Romans built the Colosseum, which could hold 50,000 people, **to show** fights between animals and gladiators.
5. For thousands of years, people have told fables **to teach** a lesson or **to pass** on wisdom.
仿写：
1. **She gets up early every morning to keep healthy.**
2. **He studies English hard to study abroad.**
3. **The children gathered together to watch the magic performance.**
4. **She practiced speaking repeatedly to get good grades in the competition.**
5. **They went to the suburbs for a picnic to relax themselves.**

**句型3：what引导的主语从句**
What started as a residential area for Chinese immigrants then turned into a centre for Chinese culture.
仿写：
1. **What makes this English speech contest special is that the winner gets a one-week study tour abroad.**
2. **What I would like to share most in this letter is my volunteer experience at the community center last summer.**
3. **What impressed me deeply is the inspiring speech given by the famous scientist in our school.**
4. **What attracts people to our school's art festival is its wide variety of performances.**

**句型4：“代词/名词+介词+关系代词”引导的定语从句**
1. The study, **in which** 1,000 students were followed for 10 years, showed that exercise helps memory.
2. The house, **in which** he grew up, has been turned into a museum.
3. My grandfather, **with whom** I shared a room when I was young, taught me to play chess.
4. The day **on which** we graduated was the happiest in my life.
5. The first time I went there was in 2015, **in which** I was deeply impressed by the local customs.
仿写：
1. **I am delighted to receive your letter, from which I know you are interested in Chinese culture.**
2. **This is the house in which I lived ten years ago.**
3. **I will always remember the day on which we met.**
4. **He escaped from the city in which he suffered so much.**
